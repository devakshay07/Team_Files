## 🔬 Research & Epistemic Methodology

All patterns documented in this layer are classified using strict epistemic standards:
- **`Observed Pattern`**: Empirically documented across $\ge 3$ independent winning projects in verified editions.
- **`Strong Recurring Pattern`**: Consistently present across $\ge 5$ winning solutions and validated against official ministry feedback.
- **`Limited Evidence / Emerging Trend`**: Observed in 1–2 recent cohorts (e.g. SIH 2024/2025 AI cohort engagements) with promising potential for SIH 2026.
