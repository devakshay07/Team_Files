# Project SNAAPP — Construction Site Photogrammetry & Progress Telemetry

## Problem Statement
- **Domain**: Computer Vision, Photogrammetry & Infrastructure Progress Auditing
- **Problem Statement ID**: `SIH2019-INFRA-01`
- **Ministry / Sector**: Steel & Heavy Infrastructure Track

## Institution / Team
- **Team Name**: Team SNAAPP
- **Institution**: Information archived in nodal records
- **Team Lead / Key Contributors**: Information archived in nodal records

## Edition
- **SIH Edition**: SIH 2019 (3rd Edition)
- **Track / Category**: Software Track
- **Prize Won**: ₹1,00,000 (1st Prize Winner)

## Official Problem Statement
Development of an automated computer vision tool to estimate physical construction progress, calculate concrete volume and steel rebar mesh density from on-site smartphone photographs, and reconcile contractor milestone billing.

## Solution
Team SNAAPP developed an offline-first mobile app using photogrammetry and contour extraction to compute structural completion percentages and store progress logs in an offline SQLite ledger that auto-syncs with enterprise ERP gateways upon reconnecting to cellular networks.

## Architecture
```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[On-Site Smartphone Photo of Steel Grid] --> B[Photogrammetry & Rebar Contour Estimator]
    B --> C[Offline SQLite Material Ledger]
    C -->|Auto-Sync on Network Reconnect| D[Enterprise RESTful Webhook Gateway]
    D --> E[Milestone Verification & Automated Contractor Payout]
```

## Technology Stack
- **Mobile Client**: Native Android (Java), OpenCV SDK
- **Backend & Middleware**: Python Django, SQLite (Mobile), PostgreSQL (Server)
- **Algorithms**: Photogrammetry, Image Segmentation & Edge Contours

## Deployment / Hardware
Mobile application engineered for field engineers in remote, low-connectivity construction zones.

## Why It Won
- `[OFFICIAL FACT]`: Declared 1st Prize winner in Heavy Infrastructure category.
- `[RESEARCH INFERENCE]`: Directly attacked the multi-crore public sector problem of ghost milestone billing and unverified contractor progress claims through automated visual photogrammetry.

# Project Wonder Bees — Indic Regional Text-to-Speech Engine

## Problem Statement
- **Domain**: Low-Resource Acoustic Synthesis, Indic NLP & Accessibility
- **Problem Statement ID**: `SIH2019-COMM-02`
- **Ministry / Domain**: Smart Communication & Accessibility

## Institution / Team
- **Team Name**: Wonder Bees
- **Institution**: Information archived in nodal records
- **Team Lead / Key Contributors**: Information archived in nodal records

## Edition
- **SIH Edition**: SIH 2019 (3rd Edition)
- **Track / Category**: Software Track
- **Prize Won**: ₹1,00,000 (1st Prize Winner)

## Official Problem Statement
Development of an ultra-lightweight Text-to-Speech synthesis engine capable of operating on legacy 512MB RAM Android phones to read government health advisories aloud in regional dialects.

## Solution
A rule-based phonetic transliteration parser combined with a concatenative audio synthesizer optimized to run locally on low-spec Android devices without consuming cellular data.

## Architecture
```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Regional Text Advisory] --> B[Rule-Based Phonetic Transliteration Engine]
    B --> C[Concatenative Waveform Synthesizer]
    C --> D[Low-Memory Audio Buffer]
    D --> E[Speaker Output on 512MB RAM Phone]
```

## Technology Stack
- **Acoustic Engine**: C++ Audio Engine, Concatenative Speech Synthesis
- **Client**: Lightweight Android App (< 8MB APK)

## Deployment / Hardware
Engineered specifically for low-cost budget smartphones (Android KitKat/Lollipop, 512MB RAM).

## Why It Won
- `[OFFICIAL FACT]`: Won 1st Prize for rural communication accessibility.
- `[RESEARCH INFERENCE]`: Proved that accessibility solutions in India must prioritize low hardware specifications and zero data costs over heavy cloud-hosted neural models.
