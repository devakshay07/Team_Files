## 🔑 1. Team Eligibility & Composition Mandates

1. **Team Size**: Exactly **6 student members** (including the designated Team Leader). Teams with fewer or more than 6 members are automatically disqualified.
2. **Gender Diversity**: **At least ONE female team member is strictly mandatory**.
3. **Institutional Affiliation**: All 6 student members must be enrolled in the **same college/institute**. Cross-college or inter-university team combinations are strictly disallowed.
4. **Academic Standing**: Open to all bona fide undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma students enrolled in AICTE/UGC-recognized higher education institutions.
5. **Submission Limit per Team**: A single team may submit solution proposals for a **maximum of 2 Problem Statements**.

---

## ⚡ 2. The 500-Idea Submission Freeze Cap

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - For SIH 2026, the submission portal enforces an automated hard quota of **500 ideas maximum** per Problem Statement.
> - The moment 500 ideas are registered for a specific Problem Statement ID across India, the portal **automatically locks that Problem Statement**, preventing any further submissions.
> - **Strategic Implication**: Do not wait until the final deadline (September 15, 2026). Complete your internal college hackathon and have your SPOC upload early before popular PS IDs lock.

---

## 🏫 3. College Quotas & Internal Hackathon Selection

* **SPOC Submission Only**: Student teams cannot submit directly on the portal. All submissions must be uploaded by the officially designated Single Point of Contact (SPOC) of the college.
* **College Nomination Quota**:
  - Maximum **50 teams** per college campus (45 Shortlisted Teams + 5 Waitlisted Teams).
  - Maximum **100 teams** per multi-disciplinary university campus.
* **Internal Hackathon Mandate**: Every institution must conduct an internal screening hackathon (evaluated by minimum 2 external judges) to shortlist their official nominations.

---

## 💰 4. Prize Structure & Travel Allowances

* **1st Prize Award**: **₹1,50,000 per Problem Statement** for the winning team (increased from ₹1,00,000 in past editions).
* **Multiple Winners / Ties**: In the event of a tie, prize allocation and division are determined solely by the evaluating Ministry / Sponsoring Department.
* **Mentors (Optional)**: Up to **2 mentors** from industry or academia with domain expertise may accompany the team (travel reimbursement provided up to ₹3,000 per person round-trip by AICTE).

---

## ⚖️ 5. Intellectual Property (IP) & Commercialization

* **50-50 IP Split**: Intellectual Property generated during SIH is **split equally (50-50)** between the student team and the Ministry / Organization that created the Problem Statement.
* **Incubation Pipeline**: Top winning teams receive direct incubation support and seed grant opportunities through MIC and national incubation centers.
